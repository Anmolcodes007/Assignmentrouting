About Netflix

Who We Are

Netflix is one of the world's leading entertainment streaming services. It provides a wide variety of movies, TV shows, documentaries, anime, and games through the internet. Netflix allows members to watch entertainment on supported devices such as smartphones, tablets, computers, smart TVs, and game consoles.

Our History

Netflix was founded in 1997 by Reed Hastings and Marc Randolph. The company originally began as a DVD rental service, sending DVDs to customers by mail. The idea was tested by mailing a DVD to themselves, which successfully arrived without being damaged.

Netflix introduced its streaming service in 2007, changing the way people watched movies and television. Over time, the company moved from primarily licensing content to producing its own original movies and series. In 2016, Netflix expanded its service to more than 130 additional countries in a single global launch.

How Netflix Works

Netflix operates as a subscription-based streaming service. Members choose a subscription plan and can stream available content over the internet. Depending on the plan and title, users can watch in different video qualities and use supported devices. Some content can also be downloaded for offline viewing.

Netflix uses recommendation technology to help users discover movies and shows based on their viewing interests. It also supports multiple profiles so members can maintain personalized viewing experiences.

Global Entertainment

Netflix has grown from a DVD rental company into a global entertainment platform. Today, Netflix says it entertains more than half a billion people in over 190 countries and 50 languages. Its content reaches audiences across different cultures and regions.

Netflix also invests heavily in local productions around the world. Its global strategy focuses on producing stories from different countries and making them available to audiences internationally.

Netflix Technology

Netflix uses large-scale technology and content-delivery systems to provide smooth streaming. Its Open Connect system works with internet service providers and places Netflix content closer to viewers, helping improve streaming performance and reduce network traffic.

Netflix also uses technology to personalize recommendations and help viewers find content they may enjoy.
